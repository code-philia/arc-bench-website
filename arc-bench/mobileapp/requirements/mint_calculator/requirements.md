# Mint Calculator
A calculator app that performs basic arithmetic operations, including addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

## REQ-1 Basic Arithmetic Operations
The calculator shall allow users to enter numbers and arithmetic operators through calculator buttons and compute results after pressing the equals button.

**Dependencies:** None

### REQ-1.1 Addition
The calculator shall support addition and display the computed sum after the user presses the equals button.

**Dependencies:** None

**Scenarios:**
- Add Two Numbers
  - **GIVEN:** The calculator is open and ready for input.
  - **WHEN:** The user presses "1", then "+", then "5", and finally "=".
  - **THEN:** The calculator displays "6".

### REQ-1.2 Subtraction
